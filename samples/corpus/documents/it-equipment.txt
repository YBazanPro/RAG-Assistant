IT Equipment Policy

Every new employee receives a laptop on the first day. Laptops are replaced every four years, or earlier when a repair would cost more than half the price of a new one.

Employees may not install software that is not in the company software catalogue. A request for new software goes to the IT service desk, which answers within five working days.

A lost or stolen laptop is reported to the IT service desk and to the security desk within one hour. The device is then locked and erased remotely.

When an employee leaves Corvane, all equipment is returned on the last working day. Equipment that is not returned is deducted from the final salary at its current value.

Personal devices may be used for email and the calendar only after the company management app is installed on them.
