# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees whose role does not require them to be on site may work from home after their probation period. Remote work is agreed with the manager and recorded in the HR system.

## Office presence

Employees working remotely spend at least two days per week in the office. Team days are set by each department.

## Equipment

Corvane provides a laptop and a headset. A one-time allowance of 150 euros covers a chair or a screen for the home office.

## Working hours and security

Remote employees keep the core hours from 10:00 to 16:00 and connect only through the company VPN. Confidential documents must not be printed at home.
