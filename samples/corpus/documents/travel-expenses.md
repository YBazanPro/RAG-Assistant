# Travel and Expense Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to every Corvane employee and contractor who travels for work or spends money on behalf of the company. It covers transport, accommodation, meals and the way expenses are claimed and reimbursed.

## Booking travel

All trips must be booked through the approved travel agency, Skylane Travel, at least 14 days before departure when possible. Train is the default for journeys under four hours. Flights in economy class are allowed for longer journeys; business class is allowed only for flights longer than eight hours and needs the approval of a department head.

## Accommodation

Hotels are booked through the agency. The nightly limit is 160 euros in most cities and 220 euros in London, Paris and Zurich, breakfast included. A more expensive room needs written approval from the manager before the booking.

## Meals and daily allowance

Meals are reimbursed up to 45 euros per day when an overnight stay is involved, and up to 20 euros for a day trip. Alcohol is never reimbursed, except at client dinners approved in advance by a director.

## Claiming expenses

Expenses are claimed in the expense tool within 30 days of the end of the trip, with a receipt for every item above 10 euros. Claims submitted later than 90 days are rejected. The manager approves the claim, and the finance team reimburses approved claims with the next monthly payroll.
