# Data Retention Policy

## Principle

Corvane keeps information only as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as long as the law requires.

## Retention periods

Accounting records and invoices are kept for ten years. Employee files are kept for five years after the employee leaves. Applications of candidates who were not hired are deleted after two years. Security logs are kept for one year. Customer support tickets are kept for three years after they are closed.

## Deletion

At the end of the retention period, information is deleted or anonymised. The data owner of each system checks once a year that deletion works.

## Legal hold

When information may be needed for a dispute or an investigation, the legal team puts it on legal hold. Information on legal hold is never deleted until the hold is lifted.
