# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees whose role does not require them to be on site may work from home after their probation period. Remote work is agreed with the manager and recorded in the HR system.

## Office presence

Since 1 March 2026, employees working remotely spend at least three days per week in the office, including the team day set by their department. Employees with a medical exemption agree their own schedule with HR.

## Equipment

Corvane provides a laptop, a headset and, on request, a second screen. A one-time allowance of 300 euros covers a chair or a desk for the home office; the receipt is claimed in the expense tool.

## Working hours and security

Remote employees keep the core hours from 10:00 to 16:00 and connect only through the company VPN. Confidential documents must not be printed at home. Working from another country is limited to 20 days per year and needs HR approval.
