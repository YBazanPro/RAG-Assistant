# Budget Exception Policy

## Purpose

A budget exception is spending that is not covered by the approved annual budget of a department. This policy says who may approve such spending.

## Approval thresholds

Exceptions up to 5,000 euros are approved by the department head. Exceptions from 5,000 to 25,000 euros also need the approval of the finance director. Exceptions above 25,000 euros are approved by the executive committee, which meets on the first Tuesday of each month.

## Request

The request is made in the finance tool. It states the amount, the reason, the expected benefit and why the cost could not be planned. Requests without a reason are returned.

## Urgent cases

When waiting for the next committee meeting would cause serious harm, the chief financial officer may approve an exception above 25,000 euros alone. The decision is reported to the committee at its next meeting.
