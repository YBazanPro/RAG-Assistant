# Information Security Incident Response

## What is an incident

A security incident is any event that may harm the confidentiality, integrity or availability of Corvane information: a lost laptop, a phishing email that was clicked, malware, an account used by someone else, or customer data sent to the wrong person.

## Reporting

Every employee reports a suspected incident immediately, and in any case within one hour, to the security desk at security@corvane.example or on the internal phone line 4400. Do not try to investigate alone, and do not delete the suspicious email.

## First response

The security desk classifies the incident as low, medium or high within two hours. For a high incident, the on-call security engineer isolates the affected devices and accounts, and the Chief Information Security Officer is informed at once.

## Personal data breaches

When personal data is involved, the Data Protection Officer decides within 48 hours whether the breach must be notified. A notifiable breach is reported to the supervisory authority within 72 hours of its discovery.

## After the incident

A short review is written for every medium or high incident within ten working days. It records the cause, the impact and the actions that prevent it from happening again.
