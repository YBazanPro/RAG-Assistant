# Meeting Room Booking

## Purpose

The offices in Lyon and Leeds have shared meeting rooms. This guide says how they are booked, so that every team can find a room when it needs one.

## Booking

Meeting rooms are booked in the room calendar of the company mail system. A meeting room can be booked for at most four hours at a time, and at most two weeks in advance. Longer events, such as training days, are booked through the facilities desk.

## Cancellation

A booking that is no longer needed is cancelled at least one hour before it starts. A room that stays empty for fifteen minutes after the start of a booking is released automatically.

## Equipment

Every room has a screen and a video conference camera. A fault is reported to the IT service desk, not to the facilities desk.
