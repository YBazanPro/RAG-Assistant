# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before the first day

The manager prepares the workstation request at least ten days before the start date. HR sends the welcome email with the address, the start time and the documents to bring.

## First day

New employees arrive at 9:30 and are welcomed by their manager and their onboarding buddy. They receive their laptop, their badge and their accounts, and they complete the security awareness training before the end of the day.

## First weeks

The onboarding buddy meets the new employee every week during the first month. Mandatory trainings on data protection and the code of conduct are completed within 30 days.

## Probation

The probation period lasts three months for employees and six months for managers. A review meeting is held with the manager two weeks before the end of probation.
