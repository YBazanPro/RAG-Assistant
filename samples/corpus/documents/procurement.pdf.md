# Procurement Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes how Corvane buys goods and services from suppliers, so that purchases are fair, documented and good value.

## Quotes

One quote is enough for purchases below 2,000 euros. Purchases from 2,000 to 20,000 euros need three written quotes. Purchases above 20,000 euros go through a tender run by the procurement team.

## Approved suppliers

Suppliers are added to the approved list only after a check of their identity, their financial health and their compliance with the supplier code of conduct. The check is renewed every two years.

## Purchase orders

No goods or services are ordered without a purchase order. Invoices without a matching purchase order are not paid and are returned to the supplier.

## Conflicts of interest

An employee who has a personal link with a supplier declares it to the procurement team and takes no part in the choice of that supplier.
